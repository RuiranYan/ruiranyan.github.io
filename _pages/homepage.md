🚀 Projects & Open Source
======
* **OmniGen / OmniGen2 - General Image Generation and Subject-Driven Data**
  * At Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence, I participated in the OmniGen series, which aims to build unified image generation models supporting text-to-image generation, image editing, and various visual tasks, while enabling cross-task knowledge transfer through a unified framework. I built a subject-driven multimodal data workflow from scratch (GroundingDINO + SAM / SAM2 + Outpaint), constructed large-scale subject-driven datasets X2I / X2I2, and used them to train OmniGen V1 / V2, significantly improving subject consistency and generation naturalness.
  * *Tags: Multimodal, Image Generation, BAAI*

* **Agent-R1 - Reinforcement Learning Framework for Agent Systems**
  * As one of the core contributors to the open-source Agent-R1 project, I helped design an end-to-end reinforcement learning training framework for agentic systems. The framework supports complex scenarios such as multi-turn conversations and multi-tool use, and adapts and refines mainstream algorithms such as PPO / GRPO to advance practical reinforcement learning for large models in real-world tasks.
  * *Tags: Agent, RL, Open Source*

* **O1Embedder - Let Retrieval Models "Think" First**
  * Traditional representation models often struggle to leverage reasoning-oriented LLMs on complex multi-step reasoning tasks. O1Embedder converts the reasoning process of large models into supervised signals by constructing long chain-of-thought data, and unifies reasoning and representation learning through multi-task learning, enabling retrieval models to achieve stronger understanding and generalization in complex reasoning and zero-shot scenarios.
  * *Tags: Representation, Reasoning*

💼 Engineering & Internship Experience
======

* **ByteDance · Douyin Search - Personalized Pre-trained Models / LLM4Rec**
  * In the multimodal pre-training direction at Douyin Search, I worked on personalized pre-trained models. By building multimodal representations, applying RQ-VAE / RQKmeans quantization tokenization, and designing representation write-back strategies, I developed video representation models adapted to the Douyin domain and applied them to ranking and long-sequence retrieval, achieving online gains on metrics such as strict-click QAUC and active-click QAUC.
  * *Tags: LLM4Rec, Multimodal*

* **OPPO · App Store - Multi-task Recommendation**
  * At OPPO, I worked on multiple rounds of optimization for multi-task recommendation models and industrial recommendation systems. In the main information-feed and app-store distribution/search scenarios, I introduced representation decoupling and auxiliary distillation mechanisms into the company's PLE-based multi-task model to mitigate negative transfer among tasks. I also optimized the Gate network structure, achieving meaningful AUC improvements and online gains while controlling model complexity.
  * *Tags: Recommender Systems, Multitask*
